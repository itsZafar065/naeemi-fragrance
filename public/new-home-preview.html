Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Naeemi Fragrances 100% Halal and alcohol-free? +
Yes, absolutely. Our attars and concentrated perfume oils are 100% free from denatured alcohol, making them purely Halal and completely safe for prayers (Namaz).
How long do these fragrances realistically last? +
Because of high perfume oil concentrations (28% to 35%), our scents stay vibrant on skin for 10–12 hours and on fabrics for over 24 hours.
Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD) across Pakistan? +
Yes! We ship all across Pakistan via courier with Cash On Delivery (COD) as well as online bank transfer / EasyPaisa. Delivery takes 2–3 working days.
What if I want to try a smaller size first? +
All our signature fragrances are offered in convenient 6ml bottles so you can try the scent before ordering our larger 12ml or 50ml editions.
